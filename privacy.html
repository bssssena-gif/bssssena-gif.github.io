🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect name, email, phone number, age, gender, and health-related information provided during registration and service usage.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to connect you with healthcare providers, process bookings, send notifications, and improve our services.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL) with encryption. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Payment Data

Payment information is processed by Razorpay and is not stored on DocNear servers. Please refer to Razorpay's privacy policy for payment data handling.

5. Cookies

DocNear uses localStorage for session management and language preferences. No third-party tracking cookies are used.

6. Data Sharing

We share your data only with healthcare providers you choose to book with. We do not share data with advertisers.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data. Contact us at support@docnear.in to exercise these rights.

8. Contact

For privacy concerns: support@docnear.in
