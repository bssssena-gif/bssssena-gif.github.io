❌ Cancellation Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. Appointment Cancellations

Patients may cancel appointments up to 2 hours before the scheduled time without any penalty. Cancellations made less than 2 hours before the appointment may be subject to a cancellation fee as per the doctor's policy.

2. Subscription Cancellations

Healthcare providers (Doctors, Medical Stores, Ambulance, Labs) may cancel their subscription at any time. The subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period. No pro-rata refunds are provided.

3. How to Cancel

  • Appointments: Login to DocNear → My Appointments → Cancel
  • Subscriptions: Contact support at support@docnear.in

4. Ambulance Bookings

Ambulance bookings cannot be cancelled once the ambulance is dispatched. Cancellations before dispatch are free of charge.

5. Lab Test Cancellations

Lab test bookings may be cancelled up to 24 hours before the scheduled test time for a full refund.

6. Contact

Cancellation queries: support@docnear.in
