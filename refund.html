💰 Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. Subscription Refunds

Subscription fees paid by healthcare providers (Doctors, Medical Stores, Ambulance, Labs) are non-refundable once the subscription is activated and access is granted.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be considered in the following cases:

  • Duplicate payment due to technical error
  • Payment charged but service not activated within 48 hours
  • Platform downtime exceeding 72 hours during subscription period

3. Refund Process

To request a refund, email support@docnear.in within 7 days of payment with your transaction ID. Approved refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days.

4. Appointment Cancellations

For appointment-related refunds, please refer to the individual doctor's cancellation policy. DocNear is not directly responsible for appointment refunds.

5. Contact

Refund queries: support@docnear.in
